The Solution

One Platform. Every Stable Operation.

Purpose-built for the unique demands of horse racing — from medication withdrawal tracking to race eligibility matching.

Medication Withdrawal Tracking

Real-time countdown timers for all 27 HISA-regulated substances. Automatic race entry blocking prevents costly violations before they happen.

Race Intelligence Engine

AI-powered eligibility matching analyzes class, distance, surface, and condition preferences. Confidence scoring helps trainers pick optimal races.

AI Document Extraction

Drop in vet reports, Coggins tests, or registration papers. Claude AI reads, categorizes, and extracts key data — eliminating manual entry.

Financial Transparency

Automatic expense tracking, invoice generation, and payout splitting by ownership percentage. Owners see exactly where every dollar goes.

Health & Compliance Hub

Vaccination schedules, injury tracking, vet records, and lab results — all in one place. Pre-race clearance checks ensure every horse is race-ready.

Owner Portal

Read-only access for syndicate owners with messaging, voting on major decisions, and full financial visibility. Builds trust and retains capital.
